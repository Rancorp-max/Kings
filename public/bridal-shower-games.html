Bridal shower games that play themselves

A quiz about the bride, a who-said-it round starring the couple, sweet advice cards and a keepsake of every guest's wishes — all on guests' phones, with nothing to print.

Start a free room →

Why move the games to phones?

Bridal showers mix guests who have known the bride since kindergarten with colleagues and future in-laws who met her last month. Phone games level that playing field. Everyone answers privately, nobody has to perform, and the live leaderboard gives the room something to cheer at. It also frees the maid of honour from printing, pencil-sharpening and score-counting, so she can actually enjoy the party she planned.

Four games to choose from

How well do you know the bride?

Multiple-choice questions about her favourites, her story and the relationship — "Who said I love you first?", "What is her signature dish?", "Where is the dream honeymoon?". Guests answer within 20 seconds on their phones, and faster correct answers earn bonus points. Use our ready-made questions or build your own in minutes.

Bride or partner: who said it?

Ask the couple a handful of questions before the shower — who hogs the blankets, who apologises first, who is the better dancer — then let guests guess. It is the round that gets the loudest reaction, and it brings the partner into the celebration even if they are not there.

Advice and story cards

In card deck mode, guests take turns drawing prompts: share one tradition the couple should steal, tell the story of how you met the bride, give a 20-second toast, or answer a would-you-rather about the wedding day. It is perfect for a relaxed brunch shower or a smaller group.

The keepsake

Any guest can leave a note during the party — a piece of advice, a wish or a prediction for the couple. The host downloads a print-ready PDF with a cover page and one card per guest. It looks lovely framed next to the guest book.

Question ideas for the bride quiz

  • Where did the couple meet?
  • Who is the better cook?
  • What is the bride's love language?
  • What is her go-to karaoke style?
  • What is her favourite flower?
  • How long did they date before getting engaged?
  • Who takes longer to get ready?
  • What is her favourite date night?
  • What was her first job?
  • Which city would she move to tomorrow?

Want it more personal? Add up to 12 facts and inside jokes, and the Party Pass writes custom questions with believable wrong answers. You can swap, edit or delete any of them before the game — nothing goes on screen that you haven't approved.

Keep it classy

Bridal showers often include grandparents and future in-laws, so our built-in content steers clear of anything suggestive, anything about exes and anything about appearance. The AI personalisation follows the same rules, with a second automatic review, and you get the final edit.

Running the shower games

  1. Open PartyDeck, choose Bridal Shower and enter the bride's name (and her partner's, if you like).
  2. Choose the quiz or the card deck, tick the correct answers, and add the who-said-it lines.
  3. At the party, show the QR code. Guests join in seconds from their own browser.
  4. Put the host screen on the TV and press start. Pause between rounds for gifts or brunch.
  5. Crown the winner on the podium and download the keepsake for the bride.

Hosting a hybrid shower? Share your screen on the video call and remote guests can play along on their phones.

No Wi-Fi at the venue?

Each guest needs a data connection, so check the signal at the venue ahead of time. If it is unreliable, run the quiz from the host screen alone and have guests call out answers, or play the card deck by passing one phone around the table.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can play?

Up to 8 players on the free plan and up to 40 with a Party Pass for the event.

Does the bride need to do anything?

Only answer a few questions in advance if you want a who-said-it round. Otherwise she can sit back and enjoy it.

Can we use it for a bachelorette too?

Yes — the bride quiz and card deck work anywhere. For an adults-only night you can also switch to the King's Cup mode (19+).

What do I print?

Nothing, unless you want the keepsake PDF at the end — it prints on standard US Letter or A4.

Ready to play?

Free for up to 8 players. No app, no sign-up — guests join with a code.
