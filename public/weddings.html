How it works

  1. Create the wedding. Add the couple, name the sides (or make your own teams) and tick the events with their dates.
  2. Guests join once. One QR code for the whole wedding. Guests pick a name, a side and an avatar and keep them — and their points — for every event.
  3. Run each event. You or a co-host runs the rounds from a phone; the big-screen page runs on the projector. Votes are counted on the server, so hundreds of guests can play at once.
  4. Finale and keepsake. The side-vs-side finale crowns the winning family, and the keepsake book collects every approved toast and blessing, grouped by event and side.

Questions

How is a wedding different from a party room?

A wedding holds several events — mehndi, sangeet, welcome party, reception — under one QR code. Guests join once and keep their name, side, avatar and points across every event and every device.

How do guests join?

They scan the wedding QR code or type the wedding code, pick a name, a side and an avatar. No app or account. If they change phone they get back in with a 6-digit guest code.

Can someone else run the games?

Yes. Add up to 5 co-hosts. Each gets a private link to run events from a phone or from the projector.

What languages are supported?

Wedding Plus includes English, Hindi, Punjabi, Urdu, Gujarati, Tamil, Spanish, French, with an option to show two languages at once.

Is anything shown on the big screen without approval?

No. AI-written questions are drafts until a host approves them, and guest messages are moderated and approved before they reach the big screen or the keepsake.

What if the venue Wi-Fi is bad?

Answers are saved on the phone and retried automatically, and phones catch up with the game as soon as they reconnect.
