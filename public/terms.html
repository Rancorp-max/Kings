Terms of use & refunds

Last updated 2026-09-29. Draft — please review before relying on it.

The service

PartyDeck provides party games played in a web browser. The free plan supports up to 8 players. A Party Pass (US$12.99, a single payment per event) unlocks the features described at checkout for 7 days from purchase.

Refunds

If the Party Pass did not work for your event, email rancorp@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you. Etsy code purchases are refunded through Etsy.

Content

Hosts are responsible for the questions, facts and notes they enter. AI-written questions are reviewed automatically but may still contain mistakes — hosts can edit or remove any question before playing.

King's Cup

King's Cup is for adults of legal drinking age (19+ in most of Canada). Drink responsibly; any sip can be a non-alcoholic drink or a light penalty. Never drink and drive.

Connectivity

Party rooms run peer-to-peer between browsers; the host must keep the game open. Some restrictive networks block these connections. Wedding games run through our servers.

Weddings and DJ/MC Pro

A Wedding Pass or Wedding Pass Plus is a single payment for one wedding, valid for 120 days. The DJ/MC Pro licence is a yearly subscription that renews automatically until cancelled from the billing portal; cancelling stops the next renewal, and weddings already created keep working until their end date. Refunds follow the policy above.
