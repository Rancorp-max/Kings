How it works

  1. Set up in minutes. Choose a theme, add the guest of honour's name and pick the quiz or the card deck. Add a few facts and we can write personalised questions for you.
  2. Guests join from their phones. Show the QR code — everyone picks an avatar and a name. No app, no account.
  3. Play together. Questions and cards appear on every phone at once, with a 20-second timer, live leaderboard, reactions and a podium.
  4. Take home a keepsake. Download a print-ready PDF of every guest's advice and wishes for the guest of honour.

Questions

Do guests need an app or account?

No. Guests open a link or scan a QR code, choose an avatar and a name, and play in their browser.

What does it cost?

Every theme is free for up to 8 players. A Party Pass (US$12.99, one payment per event) unlocks up to 40 players, personalised questions and a keepsake without watermark, valid for 7 days.

What is the keepsake?

Guests leave a piece of advice, a wish or a prediction during the party. The host downloads a print-ready PDF (US Letter or A4) with a cover and one card per guest.

Can people join remotely?

Yes — anyone with the room code can play from anywhere. Share the host screen on a video call for hybrid parties.
