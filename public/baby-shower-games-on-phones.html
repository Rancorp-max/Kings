Why phone games work so well at a baby shower

Classic paper games were designed for a living room where everyone sits in a circle. Real showers look different: guests arrive in waves, some are dialling in from another city, and the parents-to-be would rather chat than referee a pile of score cards. Phone-based games solve most of that. Guests join whenever they arrive, answers are counted instantly, and nobody has to squint at someone else's handwriting to decide whether "Theodore" and "Teddy" count as the same answer.

It also keeps the focus where it belongs. The host screen — a laptop plugged into the TV, a tablet on the mantel or just the host's own phone — shows the question and the live leaderboard, so the room reacts together. The people being celebrated can relax and enjoy it instead of running the show.

The games you can play

1. Who knows the parents-to-be best?

A fast multiple-choice quiz about the couple: where they met, who is the early riser, which baby milestone they will film first. Every guest answers on their phone within 20 seconds. Correct answers earn points, faster answers earn a bonus, and the leaderboard after each question keeps things competitive without anyone feeling put on the spot. You can use our ready-made questions, write your own, or add a handful of facts and let PartyDeck write personalised questions for you.

2. Mom or Dad: who said it?

Read out a quote or a "who is more likely to…" line and guests choose between the two parents. It is a quick round that gets big laughs, especially when the answer surprises the room. Tap the right person for each line during setup and the scoring is automatic.

3. Baby predictions

Guests guess the birth date, time of birth, weight, length, hair colour and eye colour. The predictions are saved with your party, so after the baby arrives you can enter the real details and see who got closest.

4. The advice and guessing card deck

If you prefer something gentler than a quiz, the card deck mode passes the turn around the room. Each card is a prompt: share a piece of advice for the first night home, guess the baby's first word, answer a parent-edition "would you rather", or tell a story about the parents.

A keepsake the parents will actually keep

Throughout the party, any guest can tap "Leave a note" and write a piece of advice, a wish or a prediction for the baby. At the end, the host downloads a print-ready PDF — a cover with the parents' names and the date, then one beautifully laid-out card per guest. Print it on US Letter or A4 and slip it into a frame or baby book.

How to run it, step by step

  1. Before the shower: open PartyDeck, choose "Baby Shower", type the parents' names and pick the quiz or the card deck. Tick the correct answers on the questions you want to use. This takes about ten minutes.
  2. When guests arrive: open the room and show the QR code or read out the four-letter code. Guests pick an avatar and a name — no app download, no account.
  3. During the games: put the host screen where people can see it. The quiz advances on its own, or you can pause it for a chat between questions.
  4. At the end: reveal the winner on the podium, download the keepsake PDF, and save the predictions for later.

Keeping it kind

Shower games should never make anyone uncomfortable. That is why our built-in questions avoid bodies, weight and anything that could touch on a difficult pregnancy journey, and the AI personalisation follows the same rules with an extra safety check. You can edit or delete any question before the game starts, so you always have the final word.

Printable-free alternatives if you have no Wi-Fi

Phone games need a data connection for each guest. If the venue has patchy signal, you can still use the host screen on its own: read each question aloud, let guests shout or hold up fingers for options one to four, and keep score on a whiteboard. The advice cards also work as a pass-the-phone game — one device, drawn in turn.

Frequently asked questions

Do guests need to download an app?

No. Guests open a link or scan a QR code in their phone's browser, pick an avatar and a name, and they're in.

Is it free?

Yes — every theme and the classic question sets are free for up to 8 players. A one-time Party Pass unlocks up to 40 players, personalised questions and a watermark-free keepsake for that event.

Can guests who are not in the room play?

Yes. Anyone with the link can join from anywhere, which makes it ideal for hybrid or virtual showers — just share your screen on the video call.

How long does the quiz take?

About 20 to 30 seconds per question including the reveal, so a 12-question quiz with a predictions round fits comfortably in 15 minutes.
